1. About This Place

My favorite place is a beautiful stone house in the green mountains. The house has a red tile roof and stone walls. It is surrounded by green hills, trees, and a lovely garden.

A long stone path with colorful flowers leads to the house. It is quiet, fresh, and peaceful. When I look at this picture, I feel calm and happy.

2. Why I Love It

I love this place because it is far from the noise of the city. The air is fresh, the birds sing, and the mountains are green everywhere.

I love to walk on the stone path, smell the flowers, and sit in the garden. This place makes me dream of a peaceful life with my family.

3. What You Can Do There

A Perfect Day in the Mountains

In the morning, you can walk in the garden and drink tea. In the afternoon, you can hike in the green hills and take photos. In the evening, you can sit by the house and watch the sunset over the mountains.

This is a perfect place for families, for reading, for resting, and for enjoying nature.

Interesting Facts About This Place

  1. The house is made of natural stone, so it stays cool in summer and warm in winter.
  2. The red tile roof is very old and traditional in mountain villages.
  3. The stone path is made by hand, stone by stone.
  4. The garden has red, pink, and orange flowers that bloom in spring.
  5. The green mountains around the house are full of trees and fresh air.
